Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What GATE rank is needed for IIT Bombay M.Tech Computer Science?

A: For General category candidates, an All India Rank (AIR) under 200–250 is typically required for direct M.Tech TA admission in CSE at IIT Bombay.

Q: Does IIT Bombay conduct a written test or interview for M.Tech CSE?

A: Direct admission for M.Tech TA is based purely on GATE score for top rankers. For M.Tech RA (Research Assistantship) and sponsored seats, a written test and interview may be conducted.
