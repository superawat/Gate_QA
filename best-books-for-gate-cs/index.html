Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is reading standard textbooks necessary for GATE CS?

A: Yes, standard textbooks build fundamental clarity required for conceptual MSQs and numerical answer type (NAT) questions. Combining textbook reading with PYQ solving is the most effective approach.

Q: Can I prepare for GATE CS solely using GATE PYQs?

A: Solving PYQs is mandatory, but using standard books or curated notes ensures you understand the underlying theory before attempting new question variations.
