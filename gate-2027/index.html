Overview & Analysis

The official portal for the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE 2027) has been released by the organizing institute, Indian Institute of Technology Madras (IIT Madras), at gate2027.iitm.ac.in on behalf of the National Coordination Board (NCB-GATE), Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India.

Official Status Confirmed: IIT Madras is the official organizing institute for GATE 2027. The examination will be conducted as a Computer Based Test (CBT) across 3 weekends on February 6–7, 13–14, and 20–21, 2027 in two daily shifts.

Examination Pattern & Question Types

All 30 papers in GATE 2027 follow a standardized Computer Based Test (CBT) structure:

  • Total Questions & Marks: 65 Questions carrying a total of 100 Marks over 3 Hours (180 Minutes).
  • General Aptitude (GA): 10 Questions (15 Marks) — common to all 30 papers (5 1-mark questions + 5 2-mark questions).
  • Subject Section: 55 Questions (85 Marks) covering the core subject syllabus (for engineering papers, this includes 13 marks Engineering Mathematics + 72 marks core subject).
  • Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ): 4 options with 1 correct choice. Negative marking applies: 1/3 mark deducted for 1-mark questions, 2/3 mark deducted for 2-mark questions.
  • Multiple Select Questions (MSQ): 4 options with 1 or more correct choices. No negative marking, but no partial marking (full marks only if all correct options and no incorrect options are selected).
  • Numerical Answer Type (NAT): Numeric answers entered using an on-screen virtual keypad. No negative marking.

30 Test Papers & New Paper Introduction

GATE 2027 will be conducted across 30 test papers. A new test paper, Robotics & Automation (RA), has been introduced for GATE 2027, while Textile Engineering & Fibre Science (TF) has been restructured under Engineering Sciences (XE).

Two-Paper Combinations & Examination Centres

Candidates may choose to appear in either 1 or up to 2 test papers from the approved combination matrix:

  • Computer Science (CS) Primary: Allowed secondary papers are DA, EC, GE, MA, ME, PH, RA, ST.
  • Data Science (DA) Primary: Allowed secondary papers are CS, EC, EE, MA, ME, PH, RA, ST, XE.
  • Domestic Centres Only: GATE 2027 is conducted exclusively in domestic Indian cities across 8 IIT zones. There are no international exam centres for GATE 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: When is GATE 2027 scheduled to be conducted?

A: GATE 2027 will be conducted on February 6, 7, 13, 14, 20, and 21, 2027 in two daily shifts (Forenoon: 9:30 AM – 12:30 PM; Afternoon: 2:30 PM – 5:30 PM) across three consecutive weekends.

Q: Which institute is organizing GATE 2027?

A: IIT Madras is the official organizing institute for GATE 2027 on behalf of the National Coordination Board (NCB-GATE).

Q: What is the GATE 2027 registration schedule?

A: Online registration opens on 27th August 2026. The regular registration deadline is 27th September 2026, and the extended registration deadline with late fee is 5th October 2026 on the official GOAPS portal.

Q: What is the application fee for GATE 2027?

A: For regular registration, the application fee is ₹1,000 for Female/SC/ST/PwD candidates and ₹2,000 for All Other candidates (including foreign nationals). During the extended window, the fees are ₹1,500 and ₹2,500 respectively. Two-paper candidates pay twice the single-paper fee.

Q: Is DigiLocker mandatory for GATE 2027 registration?

A: Yes, registration through DigiLocker is mandatory for all Indian nationals applying for GATE 2027. Applicants must have a verified DigiLocker account.

Q: Are there international exam centres for GATE 2027?

A: No, GATE 2027 will be conducted exclusively in domestic exam centres across India. There are no international exam centres.

Q: How long is the GATE 2027 scorecard valid?

A: The GATE 2027 scorecard is valid for exactly three years from the date of announcement of results (19th March 2027 to 18th March 2030).

Q: Can I appear in two papers in GATE 2027?

A: Yes, candidates can appear in up to two test papers from the approved combination list (e.g. Primary CS with secondary DA, EC, GE, MA, ME, PH, RA, or ST).
